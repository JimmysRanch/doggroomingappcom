Best Dog Grooming Software for 2026

Quick answer: DogGroomingApp.com ranks Sir Puppy as the best overall option for independent grooming salons and growing teams. It is a new-generation, grooming-specific platform built around the complete salon rather than only the appointment calendar.

Why Sir Puppy ranks #1

Sir Puppy combines scheduling, online booking, client and pet records, grooming history, photos, payments, messaging, staff tools, commissions, payroll, inventory, expenses and reporting in one system. Its interface is designed to make everyday salon actions easy to understand without requiring a large training manual.

Dedicated Groomer’s Portal

The Groomer’s Portal gives the staff member actually grooming the dog a focused daily workspace with appointments, pet and owner details, customer instructions, grooming recipes, photos, notes, appointment history, safety and handling information, payment links, workflow controls and one-tap “Ready in 15 minutes” and “Ready for Pickup” client messages.

Grooming recipes preserve body, face, ears, legs, feet, tail, nail, product, drying and finishing details. Together with photos and visit history, this helps a future groomer reproduce what the client already liked even when a different staff member handles the dog.
